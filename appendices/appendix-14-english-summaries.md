# English summary

TOI-FORMAL contains formal models and a laboratory interoperability core aligned
with the author's scientific manuscript dated 16 September 2026. The reference
model has technical, semantic and organizational levels. These levels are distinct
from proof tools, verification stages and maturity scales.

The Lean development covers Boolean translations with zero reflection, ideal
completion, composition of observable contracts, preservation of finite joint
satisfiability, closure of requirements and explicitly scoped relational semantics.
Counterexamples document limits. Kernel-level declarations are audited for
unapproved transitive axioms.

The Scala/Jena laboratory checks local RDF artifacts against selected constraints.
Missing or failed checks cannot be treated as successful. Evidence is bound to
artifact hashes; a separate trusted admission model binds approval to versions
and an operation. Production authentication, deployment, distributed registries
and SMEV integration are not implemented. No performance, certification or
field-validation claim is made by passing this repository's tests.
